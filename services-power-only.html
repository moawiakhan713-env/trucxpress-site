Power Only Dispatch Service

Power only means you bring the tractor and the load comes with its own trailer. You hook up to a trailer that belongs to the shipper, broker or a third-party logistics company, pull it to the receiver and drop it. No trailer payment, no trailer maintenance, and usually much less time waiting at docks.

Who power only suits

  • New owner-operators who want to start with one truck payment instead of two
  • Carriers whose trailer is in the shop and need to keep earning
  • Drivers who hate live loads, since most power only freight is drop and hook
  • Fleets with more tractors than trailers

How power only loads work

  1. The trailer is preloaded and waiting at a shipper, a drop yard or a distribution center.
  2. You hook, inspect the trailer and confirm the seal and paperwork.
  3. You deliver and drop the loaded trailer, and often pick up an empty or another loaded trailer for the return.

What you need

  • Trailer interchange insurance. Since you are pulling a trailer you do not own, most power only programs require coverage for damage to it. We check the amount each program needs.
  • The right hitch setup for the trailers you will pull, usually a standard fifth wheel.
  • Active authority and insurance that meet the broker's requirements.

Making power only profitable

Power only rates can run a little lower than the same lane with your own trailer, because you are not supplying the equipment. The profit comes from speed: drop and hook saves hours per load, so you can fit more paid miles into a week. We look for loops where you drop one trailer and hook the next nearby, keeping empty miles low.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own trailer for power only?

No. That is the point of power only. You only need a tractor that can pull the program's trailers.

What is trailer interchange insurance?

It covers physical damage to a trailer you do not own while it is in your possession. Most power only programs require it, and standard auto liability does not cover it.

Can new carriers run power only?

Some power only programs accept newer authorities and some require a few months of history. We check each program's requirements for you.

How do I get paid on power only loads?

The same way as other loads: invoice the broker or program with your paperwork, or send it to your factoring company. Payment terms vary by program.
