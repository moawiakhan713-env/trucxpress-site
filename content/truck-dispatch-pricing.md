TrucXpress charges a commission: a percentage of the line-haul rate on each load we book for you. No setup fee, no monthly subscription, no charge for weeks your truck sits. Your exact percentage depends on your equipment and fleet size, and we put it in writing before you sign anything.

## How truck dispatchers charge

Dispatch services use three pricing models. Here is how they compare for a typical owner-operator.

| Model | How it works | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Percentage | A share of each load's rate, often 5% to 10% | Carriers who want the dispatcher paid on results | What the percentage is calculated on |
| Flat weekly fee | A fixed amount per truck per week | High-mileage trucks running steady lanes | Paying in weeks the truck barely moves |
| Per load | A fixed fee for each booked load | Occasional help between your own loads | Fees that add up on short, cheap loads |

## Why we chose commission only

A flat fee gets paid whether your week was good or bad. A commission only pays when your truck moves and earns. That keeps our attention on what you care about: higher rates, fewer empty miles and brokers who pay on time. A higher-paying load earns both of us more, so we have every reason to negotiate hard.

## Questions to ask any dispatcher before you sign

1. **What is the percentage calculated on?** The line-haul rate, or the rate including fuel surcharge and accessorials?
2. **Are there other fees?** Setup, onboarding, software, per-packet or factoring referral fees all raise your real cost.
3. **Is there a contract term?** Can you leave with short notice if the service is not working?
4. **Who chooses the loads?** You should always be able to accept or refuse a load.
5. **How do they get paid?** Directly from you, or deducted somewhere you cannot see?

> Our answers: commission on the load rate, no other fees, no long-term contract, you approve every load, and every charge shows up on your statement.

## Frequently asked questions

### What percentage does TrucXpress charge?
It depends on your equipment type and how many trucks you run. Request a quote and we will send your exact percentage in writing. There are no setup or subscription fees on top of it.

### Do I pay if my truck has a slow week?
No. Commission is only charged on loads we book for you. If we do not book, you do not pay.

### Can I book some loads myself?
Yes. Many carriers keep a few direct customers or their own lanes. We only charge commission on loads we book.

### Is a percentage more expensive than a flat fee?
It depends on your gross. In strong weeks a percentage costs more dollars than a flat fee; in slow weeks and weeks off it costs less or nothing. Divide the flat fee by the percentage to find your break-even week. Our guide on [percentage vs flat fee](blog-dispatch-percentage-vs-flat-fee.html) walks through a full-year example.
