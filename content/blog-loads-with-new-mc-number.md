Your authority is active, your insurance is paid, and the truck is ready. Then you call brokers and hear the same thing: "Call us back when you have 90 days." Every new carrier hits this wall. Here is how to get past it.

## Why brokers say no to new MCs

Brokers are responsible for the freight they hand over, and fraud has made them cautious. Authority age is an easy filter, so many set a minimum of 90 days, six months or more. It is not personal, and it is not universal. A meaningful number of brokers onboard new carriers if the paperwork, insurance and identity checks are clean.

## Five ways to find loads in your first 90 days

1. **Target brokers that onboard new authorities.** They exist, but they are scattered. Ask directly, keep a list, and note each one's insurance requirements.
2. **Use load boards with a filter mindset.** Large boards list thousands of loads. Many postings include notes on authority age. Call the ones without restrictions first.
3. **Look at power only.** If you have a tractor, power only programs move preloaded trailers and some accept newer carriers. See our [power only guide](blog-power-only-trucking-explained.html).
4. **Talk to shippers directly.** Local manufacturers, distributors and building suppliers sometimes need a reliable truck and care more about service than authority age.
5. **Work with a dispatcher who already has the list.** A dispatcher who works with new MCs every week saves you a month of cold calls.

## Make your carrier profile easy to approve

- Get your certificate of insurance ready with the limits brokers expect.
- Keep a clean, complete [carrier setup packet](blog-carrier-setup-packet.html) you can send in minutes.
- Answer the phone and reply to emails fast. New carriers win loads on responsiveness.
- Make sure your FMCSA contact details are correct and match your email and phone.

## Build history fast

Your first loads are an investment. Choose reliable brokers, be early for pickups, send updates without being asked and get clean delivery paperwork. A spotless first 60 days opens the door to brokers that pay better.

## Avoid the traps

- **Scammers target new carriers.** An unusually high rate from a broker you cannot verify is a red flag. Read our post on [double brokering red flags](blog-avoid-double-brokering.html).
- **Do not take every load.** A cheap load into a dead market can cost you two days and a long deadhead out.
- **Watch cash flow.** Brokers often pay in 30 days or more. Plan for it, or look at factoring carefully.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do all brokers require 90 days of authority?
No. Many do, but some onboard new carriers immediately when insurance and documents check out.

### Can I use a load board on day one?
Yes. Load boards do not require authority age, though individual postings may. Filter and call the loads without restrictions first.

### Should I lease onto another carrier instead?
Leasing on avoids broker restrictions but gives up independence and part of your revenue. Many new carriers prefer to keep their own authority and use a dispatcher to get through the first 90 days.
