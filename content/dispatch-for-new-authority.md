The first 90 days with a new authority are the hardest stretch in trucking. Many brokers will not load a carrier until the MC is a few months old, insurance costs are at their highest, and every empty day still has a truck payment attached. TrucXpress dispatches for new authorities on purpose. We know which brokers will work with you now, and we keep your truck loaded while your history builds.

## Why new authorities struggle to get loads

Brokers carry the risk when a load goes wrong, so many of them screen carriers by how long the authority has been active. A common rule is 90 days, some ask for six months or a year, and a growing group will onboard a carrier on day one if the paperwork and insurance check out. The problem is that a new carrier has no easy way to tell those groups apart, so most of the first month goes into calling brokers who were never going to say yes.

New carriers also run into:

- **Setup friction.** Every broker wants its own carrier packet, insurance certificate and verification steps before the first load.
- **No payment history.** Factoring companies and brokers have little to go on, which can slow approvals.
- **Rate pressure.** A carrier who needs any load to make a payment ends up accepting the lowest rate on the board.

## How we dispatch for new MCs

1. **Readiness check.** We confirm your authority status, USDOT and MC details, insurance limits and the documents brokers will ask for.
2. **A broker list that fits you.** We work from brokers who onboard new authorities, and we vet each one for authority, bond and payment history before we book.
3. **Setup packets done for you.** We fill out and submit carrier packets so you are approved before a good load comes up, not after.
4. **First loads that build history.** We look for clean, on-time loads with reliable brokers, because a strong first month opens doors to better-paying freight.
5. **Rate growth.** As your authority ages and your on-time record grows, we move you onto lanes and brokers that pay more.

## What you need before we start

- Active operating authority (we can help you check it)
- Certificate of insurance with auto liability and cargo coverage
- W-9 for the business
- Voided check or a factoring Notice of Assignment for payment
- Your equipment type, home base and the lanes you want to run

Not active yet? Start anyway. We will tell you what is missing and what brokers in your equipment type usually require.

> Commission only means we earn a percentage of the loads we book for you. If your truck does not move, you do not pay us.

## Mistakes that cost new carriers money

- **Taking every load offered.** Cheap loads into weak freight markets leave you stranded and deadheading out.
- **Skipping broker checks.** New carriers are the main target of double brokering scams. We verify every broker before you sign a rate confirmation.
- **Waiting on paperwork.** A missing insurance certificate can cost you the best load of the week.
- **No plan for the way home.** We plan the reload before you take the load, so one good rate does not turn into two empty days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I get loads with a brand-new MC number?
Yes. A smaller group of brokers onboards new authorities right away, and load boards plus power-only programs also have options. The first weeks usually pay less than they will later, which is why choosing reliable brokers for history matters more than chasing the highest rate.

### Do you charge new carriers an upfront or setup fee?
No. We work on commission only, a percentage of each load we book. There is no setup fee and no monthly subscription.

### How long until brokers stop treating me as new?
Most broker requirements fall away between 90 days and six months of active authority with a clean record. On-time pickups, clean paperwork and no claims make that window shorter.

### What insurance do brokers usually ask for?
Most brokers ask for at least $1,000,000 in auto liability and $100,000 in cargo coverage, even though the federal minimum for general freight is lower. Some freight types require more. We check each broker's requirements before booking.
